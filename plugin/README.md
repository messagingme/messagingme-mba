# Messaging Me for Claude Code

WhatsApp for your app, from Claude Code: your WhatsApp number, an AI agent that answers your customers and a
chat bubble for your site, set up in one session with [Messaging Me](https://app.messagingme.fr/en/developers/).

**No account to create beforehand, no key to copy. No WhatsApp number? We provide one. No Meta Business account? Meta's window
creates it along the way, and you can keep your WhatsApp Business app.**

[![The plugin installed, the workspace authorized, the number connected and a first message received, in Claude Code](https://app.messagingme.fr/img/plugin-demo.gif)](https://app.messagingme.fr/en/developers/)

## Install

In Claude Code:

```
/plugin marketplace add https://github.com/messagingme/messagingme-plugin.git
/plugin install messagingme@messagingme
```

In Codex, the same plugin, in a terminal:

```
codex plugin marketplace add https://github.com/messagingme/messagingme-plugin.git
codex plugin add messagingme@messagingme
```

## Which WhatsApp number?

This is where most people get lost, so here are the words first, then the two ways to set up your WhatsApp account.

- **Meta Business account**: your business's account at Meta (a "business portfolio", formerly Business Manager), on
  business.facebook.com. It is free; if you advertise on Facebook or Instagram, you probably have one. Otherwise,
  nothing to prepare: with your Facebook profile, Meta's window has you create it during the setup (business name,
  email, country, website).
- **WhatsApp Business app**: the free app for businesses, on your phone, distinct from WhatsApp.

Two ways to set up your WhatsApp account:

- **Keep your WhatsApp Business app**: we transform it slightly so that messages can be automated. You still reply
  from the app on your phone, and you also automate whatever you want with Messaging Me. The name and photo are set in
  the app, with no business name verified by Meta. What you type in the app does not show in Messaging Me yet, and you
  need to open the app at least every two weeks.
- **Create an API number directly**: a number connected through Meta to the official WhatsApp API, driven entirely by
  Messaging Me. Your business's name is displayed, reviewed by Meta; everything goes through Messaging Me (automatic
  replies, campaigns, a shared Inbox); nothing to keep open on a phone. Meta bills the templates and the replies
  beyond a monthly allowance, on a payment method you add at Meta or on the credit line.

Either way, Claude asks you a few questions to find what you want and how.

Then `/messagingme:demarrer` asks you, one question at a time, the same questions as the
[Get started](https://app.messagingme.fr/en/get-started/) page:

1. **Which plan?** Free, driven entirely from Claude Code, or Pro, which adds the console and includes a provided
   number.
2. **How do you want to pay for your Meta messages?** A payment method (Visa / Mastercard) on your Meta Business
   account, which Meta bills for your templates; or the credit line, prepaid at Messaging Me with no card at Meta, with
   the Pro or Business plan.
3. **Do you have a Meta Business account?** Yes: your number becomes an API number, an available one of yours or a
   provided one; if it is already in the WhatsApp Business app, you can keep it. No, or not sure: the next question
   (business.facebook.com lists your portfolios, and a missing account is created along the way).
4. **Do you already have the WhatsApp Business app?** (Its logo: WhatsApp's green bubble, marked with a B.) Yes: **Do
   you already have a number working in that app?** If so, **Do you want to use that number?** Using it, you keep the
   app. Otherwise, or with no app, the next question.
5. **How do you want to use WhatsApp?** With the WhatsApp Business app: you also reply from your phone, and your AI
   agent replies from Messaging Me; the name and photo are set in the app, which loses its broadcast lists and away
   message, and you pay Meta with your card, no credit line. For another number than the one in your app, that is a
   2nd account in it, two at most per app; with no app yet, you first install it on your phone, for free. Or directly
   through the official API: everything goes through Messaging Me (automatic replies, campaigns; with Pro, a shared
   Inbox), and the display name is customized at Meta, which reviews it.
6. With the app, **Would you like us to provide a number?** Yes: we provide one, which you put in your app, its
   verification code shown on the setup page. No: you put a number of yours in the app. Either way, Meta's window
   then connects the app, and you keep it.
7. Through the API, **Do you already have a number you could use?** Yes: your own number becomes your API number,
   connected through Meta's window, which creates your Meta Business account along the way. No: we provide one,
   connected the same way, its verification code shown on the setup page.

The price of a provided number is under [What it costs](#what-it-costs).

## Update

The plugin does not update on its own. In Claude Code: `/plugin`, Marketplaces tab, `messagingme`, "Update marketplace"
(or "Enable auto-update"). In a terminal: `claude plugin update messagingme@messagingme`; on Windows, in PowerShell,
type `claude.cmd` instead of `claude` (PowerShell blocks the `claude.ps1` script). The new version loads in the next
session, or after `/reload-plugins`.

If you had added the same server by hand before (same address, any name), Claude Code keeps yours and sets the
plugin's aside: no duplicate tools, your authorization stays valid. To switch to the plugin's, remove the old one with
`claude mcp remove` followed by its name.

## Data and privacy

The plugin runs no code on your machine: it contains instructions for Claude (skills and a command) and declares one
remote server, the Messaging Me MCP server at `https://api.messagingme.app/mcp`. It talks to nothing else.

- **Authorization.** You sign in to Messaging Me in your browser (OAuth), as an administrator of your workspace. No key
  or secret is stored in your files, and you can revoke the access from the console at any time.
- **What is sent.** When Claude calls a tool at your request, the server receives that call and its arguments (for
  example the text of a message to send, the settings of an AI agent, or the address of a website to import as
  knowledge). It does not receive your conversations with Claude.
- **What comes back.** The data of your workspace that the tool returns: conversations and messages, contacts,
  templates, AI agents, statistics. Actions that change your workspace (sending a message, activating an agent,
  opening a payment page) are marked as such, and payments always happen on Stripe's page, by you.
- **Before you start.** A Messaging Me account (created during the setup). For the official API, a Facebook profile:
  Meta's window offers to create your Meta Business account (a business portfolio, in Meta's screens) during the setup;
  messages that Meta bills are charged on its card, except with the credit line, prepaid at Messaging Me. To keep your WhatsApp Business app, the app on your phone.
