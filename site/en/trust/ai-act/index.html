Trust center The AI Act, in detail.

The Messaging Me AI agent answers your customers on WhatsApp. Here is who is accountable for it, what your customer sees, and what stays under human control.

Roles, and the level of risk

WhoRole under the AI Act
Messaging MeProvides the AI agent
YouDeploy the agent with your customers
Intended useCustomer relations, a limited-risk use

Transparency

Your customer knows they are talking to an AI: by default, the platform itself adds the AI disclosure to the conversation.

Human oversight

  • The agent hands over to a human when needed.
  • Your team takes the conversation back in the inbox.
  • The agent’s actions and tool calls are logged.

Safeguards

  • Your customer’s data is delimited in the agent’s prompt, never mixed with its instructions.
  • The agent’s tools are taken from a closed list.
  • Its outputs are validated before acting.
  • Its credit is capped.

Your data

None of our customers’ data is used to train the models.
