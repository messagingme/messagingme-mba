Guides RCS, SMS or WhatsApp: which channel for your messages?

What each channel can do, who it reaches, what it costs and what consent it requires. And how to stop choosing.

Updated on 24 November 2026

The short answer

SMS reaches everyone, but only carries text. RCS adds your brand’s logo, images, carousels and buttons, in the phone’s Messages app, with nothing to install. WhatsApp is the channel for conversation: customer service, appointment booking, AI agent, order tracking.

For a campaign, the right choice is often not one channel but two: each customer gets the message on the channel they have.

SMS: universal, but text only

SMS is still the only channel every mobile phone receives, even without a data connection. That’s its strength for alerts, verification codes and urgent messages.

Its limit: text and a link, a simple sender name, and little measurement beyond the click. For an offer that sells through images, it converts less well than a rich channel.

RCS: rich SMS, in the Messages app

RCS is the rich messaging of the phone’s messages app. The recipient installs nothing: the message lands where their SMS do, but under your brand’s name and logo.

An RCS message carries text, an image and buttons, or a carousel of scrolling cards. There is no template to get approved: you write, you save, you send.

Its limit: it only reaches compatible phones with rich messaging turned on. In Messaging Me, RCS is part of the Enterprise plan. Compose and send an RCS message, step by step.

WhatsApp: the channel for conversation

WhatsApp is the app where your customers already write to their friends and family. That’s where a campaign becomes a conversation: a question, an appointment, a payment, an exchange with your team or your AI agent.

Its rule: you can only write freely to someone who wrote to you in the last 24 hours. To write first, as in a campaign, you need a Meta-approved template, classified as utility (an order, an appointment, an account) or marketing (everything else). The 24-hour window, in detail.

What each channel costs

SMS and RCS are paid per message, to their provider. WhatsApp is billed by Meta, directly to your business, according to its rate card by country and message category, except with the credit line (Pro or Business plan), prepaid at Messaging Me. In France, a reply to a customer costs €0.0248, with 1,000 free replies per month and per number, and a marketing template costs more. Messages your customers send you are never billed. Meta’s rate card.

Comparing the price of a message isn’t enough: what counts is the cost of a reply or a sale. Messaging Me shows the cost per engagement of each campaign and the cost per lead of each ad. Cost control.

Consent, channel by channel

Every marketing channel requires explicit consent. Consent collected for SMS does not cover WhatsApp: collect it for WhatsApp, and make your first message a clear confirmation, with an easy opt-out.

In Messaging Me, a contact who replies STOP is no longer contacted: the check runs before every send, whether it comes from a campaign, a scenario, the AI agent or your team. GDPR at Messaging Me.

Which channel for which use

  • Verification code, urgent alert: SMS.
  • Visual offer, new collection, in-store invitation: RCS, or WhatsApp for customers who accepted it.
  • Appointment confirmation and reminder, order tracking: WhatsApp utility, with RCS fallback.
  • Customer service, pre-purchase questions: WhatsApp, with an AI agent and handover to your team.
  • Ad that opens a conversation: Click-to-WhatsApp.

Stop choosing: WhatsApp and RCS, with fallback

When you create a campaign in Messaging Me, the “WhatsApp and RCS, with fallback” option sends on the chosen channel first. If the send fails, the message goes out right away on the other channel: each customer gets one message. Whether they reply on WhatsApp or RCS, your team answers in the same Inbox.

Frequently asked questions.

What is the difference between SMS and RCS?

SMS carries text and a link. RCS lands in the same place, in the Messages app, but under the brand’s name and logo, with images, carousels and buttons.

Do you need to install an app to receive RCS?

No. RCS arrives in the phone’s messages app, as long as the phone is compatible and rich messaging is turned on.

RCS or WhatsApp: which one for a campaign?

RCS for a visual message, with no template to get approved. WhatsApp to open a conversation with customers who accepted your messages. With fallback between the two, each customer gets the message on the channel they have.

Can a customer receive the same message twice with fallback?

No. Fallback only goes out if the first send fails. Each customer gets one message.

How much does a WhatsApp message cost in France?

Meta bills a reply to a customer €0.0248, with 1,000 free replies per month and per number. Marketing templates cost more, according to Meta’s rate card.

Is consent collected for SMS enough to message on WhatsApp?

No. Collect consent for WhatsApp, and remind people of it in your first message, with an easy opt-out.
