Three steps, one session.

Claude Code

  1. 1

    Install the plugin

    In Claude Code, one after the other. No account to create beforehand, no key to copy.

    /plugin marketplace add https://github.com/messagingme/messagingme-plugin.git
    /plugin install messagingme@messagingme
  2. 2

    Authorize

    Type /mcp, pick plugin:messagingme:messagingme, then Authenticate. On the page that opens, “Continue with Google”, “Create my workspace with my email address” (through the link we email you) or, if you already have an account, your password: with no account yet, your workspace is created along the way. One click on “Authorize”, and Claude takes it from there.

  3. 3

    Start

    Claude reads where your workspace stands, then takes you to your WhatsApp number: your own, or one we provide. That is when Meta asks for your free Meta Business account, which its window has you create along the way if you lack one (the step-by-step guide). Then an AI agent that answers your customers and a bubble on your site. An interrupted session resumes where it stopped.

    /messagingme:demarrer

Codex

  1. 1

    Install the plugin

    codex plugin marketplace add https://github.com/messagingme/messagingme-plugin.git
    codex plugin add messagingme@messagingme
  2. 2

    Authorize

    In a terminal. The same page opens: Google, your email address or your password, then “Authorize”. It says “unverified”: that is expected.

    codex mcp login messagingme
  3. 3

    Start

    In Codex, ask for the setup: it reads where your workspace stands, takes you to your WhatsApp number, then to the AI agent and the bubble on your site.

    Set up WhatsApp with Messaging Me.

Cursor, VS Code or Lovable: add the MCP server https://api.messagingme.app/mcp, and the same authorization opens.

Frequently asked questions.

Is this the official WhatsApp API?

Yes: Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform. Messaging Me is a Meta Tech Provider, and the WhatsApp Business account lives in your Meta Business account (formerly Business Manager) and stays yours. No Meta Business account yet? Meta’s window has you create it along the way.

I have no Meta Business account: can I still use it?

Yes. Meta’s window has you create your Meta Business account along the way, with your Facebook profile (your business name, an email address, the country and the website), then offers a payment method. Already have the WhatsApp Business app? Keep it: its number also sends from Messaging Me, and you still reply from your phone. No number in the app, or you would rather not use the one it has? Put a number of yours in it, or the one we provide (a WhatsApp Business app holds two accounts at most), then keep the app the same way; or go directly through the official API, with your number or ours. No app? You do not need it: Meta’s window connects your available number to the official API or, without a number, the one we provide. To also reply from your phone, first install WhatsApp Business: it is free. A provided number’s verification code shows on the Messaging Me page. Is your number used in WhatsApp, the regular app? Install WhatsApp Business on the same phone: it offers to take over that number, with your chats. Then keep WhatsApp Business the same way.

Which WhatsApp number should I use?

Your number goes through the official WhatsApp API, connected through Meta. It lets you write first to your customers (confirmations, reminders, campaigns, Shopify messages) with templates, which Meta approves and then bills you for. Three cases.
1) A number used in no WhatsApp app: it becomes your API number.
2) The number of your WhatsApp Business app: you keep it in the app on your phone, and it also sends from Messaging Me. Is your number used in WhatsApp, the regular app? Install WhatsApp Business on the same phone: it offers to take over that number, with your chats.
3) No number: we provide one (€3.50 a month, included in Pro), connected through Meta’s window or, if you choose the WhatsApp Business app, put in the app, which you then keep the same way.
Claude helps you choose when you connect the number.

What do I need at Meta before I start?

1) A Meta Business account: formerly Business Manager, which Meta now calls a “business portfolio” in its screens. It will hold your WhatsApp account, your number and your card. You sign in with your Facebook profile, or with a managed Meta account from your organization.
2) No Meta Business account yet? Nothing to prepare: Meta’s window has you create it along the way, with your Facebook profile (your business name, an email address, the country and the website), then offers a payment method. You can also create it beforehand on business.facebook.com: open the menu under “Home” at the top left, then create a business portfolio.
3) A spare number, not used in any WhatsApp app, or the number of your WhatsApp Business app, which stays in it: you keep the app with “Open the Meta window”. Is your number used in WhatsApp, the regular app? Install WhatsApp Business on the same phone: it offers to take over that number, with your chats. Meta sends a code to your number, by text message or phone call: keep the phone that holds it at hand.
No number? We provide one!

My name or photo does not show on WhatsApp.

That is your number’s display name at Meta, separate from your workspace name. Where to change it depends on your number.
Do you keep the WhatsApp Business app? The name and photo can be changed neither in Business Manager nor in Messaging Me: Meta leaves them to the app. Change them in its business profile, on your phone.
Is your number connected directly to the API, without the app? The name and photo are changed in your Business Manager:
1) Open WhatsApp Manager: business.facebook.com/wa/manage/home/ (or the ≡ menu at the bottom left, “All tools”, then “WhatsApp Manager”).
2) Click your number, under your WhatsApp account (or “Account tools”, then “Phone numbers”).
3) “Profile” tab: “Edit” under “Display name” for the name, the button under “Profile picture” for the photo.
Meta reviews every new name (ten changes at most per 30-day period): during the review, or after a rejection, WhatsApp may show the number instead.
Name approved? Write to us: Meta requires the number to be re-registered within 14 days for the new name to show. The guide with screenshots: app.messagingme.fr/en/help/change-my-number-name-and-photo/

My message was not sent.

After 24 hours without a message from the person, only a template approved by Meta can be sent (send_template_to_contact). Within 24 hours of their last message, a free-form reply goes out (reply_in_open_window). get_conversation tells whether the window is open.

Why does Meta ask for a card, and what does it charge?

With the Messaging Me credit line, no card at Meta. You prepay it with us, from Claude before connecting the number, or in the console (Plan, Credit line tab), with the Pro or Business plan (not with the WhatsApp Business app), and your WhatsApp messages are paid from it.
Meta bills the payment method of your Meta Business account, except with the credit line: the templates and, since October 1, 2026, the replies beyond a monthly allowance, according to its rate card and the person’s country. No plan, Free included, includes these fees.
Claude gives you the current price of a message delivered in France, category by category, read from Meta’s rate card (list_templates); for another country, Meta’s rate card: developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing.
To add the card:
1) On business.facebook.com, open WhatsApp Manager, “Overview” page.
2) On your WhatsApp account, click the three dots, then “Manage account settings”.
3) In the “Settings” tab, open “Payment settings”, then “Add payment method”.
4) Enter a Visa or Mastercard card (Meta accepts neither American Express nor PayPal), then your business details; Meta may ask for your tax information.
5) You must own the WhatsApp account, or have the “Manage WhatsApp account” permission. Without a valid card, only replies within the 24-hour window go out: templates (campaigns, follow-ups) are blocked.

Utility or marketing: what decides?

What the message is for, not its tone. Utility: no promotion at all, and about something the person did or asked for (their order, booking, account, payment, the confirmation of their sign-up), factual and specific. Marketing: everything else, an offer, a promo code, something new, a newsletter, a welcome that presents the business, an abandoned cart reminder, a general survey; a template that mixes order information and a promotion is marketing. Marketing costs more: Claude tells you the category and its price before submitting a template. A template declared utility that is not is approved as marketing by Meta, and repeated misuse limits the account’s utility templates.

My template was rejected.

Claude reads the rejection reason (get_template_status), suggests a corrected version and submits it again (create_template). Meta may also change a template’s category: list_templates shows the one that applies.

My AI agent stopped answering.

Four causes. The AI credit is used up: get_credit, then buy_credit. The agent was deactivated, or is no longer the one answering: get_setup_state tells. Someone replied by hand, Claude included (reply_in_open_window): replying takes over the conversation, and the agent stays silent until it gets it back, on its own after a delay without a new reply, or right away: control_conversation hands it back from Claude, or “Hand back” in the console Inbox. And on Free, a new customer who writes to a workspace that already has its 100 contacts does not get in, so the agent does not answer them: get_plan shows the count.

Can my AI agent act in my application?

Yes, through an action: set_agent_action adds it to the agent, with an instruction (when to trigger it, what to tell the customer next) and what it collects (a contract number, a date, a reason), all optional. When the customer makes the request, the agent answers what the instruction says and carries on, and your application receives the agent.action webhook, at most once a day per contact and per action. This type is unchecked by default: add it to the address that already serves your application (update_webhook_endpoint, or the console, Developers > Webhooks sortants), or create one (create_webhook_endpoint). Without an application, a console automation can start from the action.

The verification code does not arrive.

With a number provided by Messaging Me, the code is captured for you: it shows on the connection page, and Claude shows it too (watch_whatsapp_connection). The path in a video (3 minutes): app.messagingme.fr/en/help/get-a-number-provided-by-messaging-me/
With a provided number put in your WhatsApp Business app, its code shows the same way: you type it in the app, not in Meta’s window.
With your own number, ask for a call rather than an SMS when Meta offers the choice. The path in a video: app.messagingme.fr/en/help/connect-my-whatsapp-number/
With your own number in the WhatsApp Business app, Meta sends the code to that number, by text message or phone call: keep the phone at hand. The path in a video: app.messagingme.fr/en/help/keep-my-whatsapp-business-app/
Meta allows only ten code requests per number over 72 hours.

Can I keep this number in the WhatsApp app?

Yes, with the WhatsApp Business app: the same number stays in the app on your phone and also goes through the API, if you want: this is what Meta calls “coexistence” (the “Open the Meta window” button of the “Connect WhatsApp” page, in the console or through Claude’s link). A number we provide can go there too: you add it in the app, its verification code shows on the connection page, then you keep the app the same way. You keep replying from your phone, but what you type there does not show in Messaging Me yet. A few app features stop (broadcast lists, away message), and you need to open the app at least every two weeks. You pay Meta with your card: the credit line is not possible with the app. Is your number used in WhatsApp, the regular app? Install WhatsApp Business on the same phone: it offers to take over that number, with your chats. Then keep WhatsApp Business the same way.

Do I own the provided number?

No: it is rented for €3.50 a month, included in Pro. During the Pro trial, it costs €3.50, deducted from your first Pro plan invoice, and not refunded if you do not keep it at the end of the trial. A number you bring stays yours.

How many messages can I send?

Three limits. The plan’s: 1,000 templates a month on Free, unlimited on Pro; replies within the 24-hour window never count. The API’s: a daily quota per workspace, set by the plan, where templates and replies are counted separately (sending templates never takes the room of replies), raised on request; beyond it, the quota_exceeded refusal until the next day. And Meta’s, which sets what a number can send and raises it with its quality.

What happens when my Free workspace reaches 100 contacts?

On Free, all your contacts count, those who write to you first included. Once the workspace is full, creating or importing a contact is refused (plan_limit_reached), and a new customer who writes to you does not get in: no record, no conversation, no reply from your AI agent. An email lets you know, one a day at most. If that customer wrote STOP, their refusal is still kept. Your existing contacts write and receive as before. To make room, ask Claude to delete the contacts you no longer need, in bulk: the oldest, those with a tag, by consent or from a list (prepare_contacts_deletion, then delete_contacts). It first shows you how many and a few records, then deletes on your go-ahead, 1,000 at most at a time; the deletion is permanent. Or move to Pro, which allows 1,000, those who write to you included.

Can Claude prepare and launch a campaign?

Yes, on Pro, and nothing goes out without your yes. Claude finds the contacts (find_contacts), turns them into a segment, which is a tag (tag_contacts), then prepares the campaign (prepare_campaign): a draft visible in the console, with a summary (contacts targeted, how many will receive it, those left out and why, 5 names, the number of messages Meta bills) and a code valid for 30 minutes. It only launches (launch_campaign) after your yes, with that code; a chosen date schedules the send. You can also launch the draft from the console. The template must be approved, and a marketing template only reaches contacts who opted in.

How do I add a colleague?

From the console, with an invitation. 1 user on Free, 3 on Pro then €15 per extra user per month (offered when inviting), 5 on Business then €10 per extra user per month (asked from Support), per quote on Enterprise.

How do I cancel, and what happens to the number?

Everything is cancelled in the Stripe portal. The provided number paid on its own (€3.50 a month): ask Claude, which opens the portal (manage_number_subscription), or in the console, Plan page or "Connect WhatsApp" page, number subscription button. Pro: Plan page, "Cancel my subscription". A cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid, with no refund. The number keeps sending until then; after that its sends stop, resubscribing within 7 days gives back the same number, then it is released and leaves the workspace. When Pro ends, the provided number moves to €3.50 a month on the same card, unless you choose to give it back (return_number_at_plan_end).

How do I update the plugin?

It does not update on its own. In Claude Code: /plugin, Marketplaces tab, messagingme, “Update marketplace” (or “Enable auto-update” from then on). In a terminal: claude plugin update messagingme@messagingme; on Windows, in PowerShell, type claude.cmd instead of claude (PowerShell blocks the claude.ps1 script). The new code loads in the next session, or after /reload-plugins.

I had already added the server by hand: what now?

Connected without a key (authorized through /mcp): nothing to do. Claude Code keeps yours and sets the plugin’s aside, with no duplicate tools. Connected with an API key: remove it (claude mcp remove followed by its name). A key does not open the setup tools, and yet Claude Code would keep that server.
