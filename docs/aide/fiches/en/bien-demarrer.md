# Getting started

When you first sign in, the console shows you the five steps that put WhatsApp to work for you, and goes with you
through each one. You have nothing to tick: it sees by itself what your workspace has already done.

**1. Five steps to get started.** The welcome window lists the steps: connect your WhatsApp number, connect your store
or your CRM, a first scenario or a first campaign, an AI agent that answers, the WhatsApp bubble on your website. Each
remaining step has its button, and the next one is in blue. The window's film (“The film, 1 min”) shows the whole way.

**2. Connect your store or your CRM.** “Shopify” opens Integrations, and a card, at the top right, goes with you: it
says the current step, frames the field or button to press, and most of the time moves on by itself as soon as it is
done (a step that explains waits for “Next”). “Another tool” takes you to Integrations, without a card.

**3. A scenario or a campaign.** “A campaign” starts with your contacts (to import, or to add by hand), then the
campaign assistant takes you from the name to the launch, one step at a time. “A scenario” has you create the
scenario, build it block by block, then publish it; it is only offered if your plan includes scenarios.

**4. An AI agent that answers.** “Guide me” takes you to create your agent, then keeps you in “Build by talking”: you
describe your business, the assistant writes the agent for you. The other tabs are for refining it later. At the end,
“Save and activate”.

**5. The WhatsApp bubble on your website.** “Add” opens the bubble page: “New widget”, then paste its tag on your
website, and your visitors message you on WhatsApp in one click.

**6. Pick up where you left off.** Once the window is closed, the “Getting started” card at the top of Home says where
you are; “Continue” reopens the steps, as does “Getting started” at the top of the help panel. The card disappears
once the five steps are done.

**Good to know.** The card that goes with you blocks nothing: the console stays clickable underneath. Its collapse
button folds it into a badge, the cross stops it, and if you wander off the step's screen, “Go there” brings you
back. It lasts as long as the tab stays open, even after a reload. Only an admin sees the steps: their screens are
reserved for admins. Another member gets the console tour. On the Free plan, without a console, none of this shows.
