# Connect my WhatsApp number, and read its status

As long as no number is attached, the Home screen offers “Continue the setup”: the “Connect WhatsApp” page guides
you in five steps, one question at a time.

**1. Choose your plan.** Free, at no cost, is run from Claude Code or Codex. The Pro plan, at €49 a month, opens the
whole console and starts with a 14-day free trial: you subscribe on Stripe's page, then come back to the next step.
Business and Enterprise are requested through the contact form.

**2. Choose how to pay for your Meta messages.** Meta bills each template you send. Two solutions: you pay Meta
yourself, with a payment method on your Meta Business account, or Messaging Me advances these costs for you, with the
credit line. Nothing is chosen in advance: the next step only appears once you have answered.

No card is needed to connect your number: the payment method at Meta is added afterwards, on your Meta Business
account, before your first templates. See “Pay Meta: add your card”.
The credit line costs €30 per number per month, with the Pro or Business plan: you top it up by card in Messaging Me,
and on Enterprise it is invoiced. See “Pay for your messages without a card at Meta”. A number that keeps the
WhatsApp Business app stays on your payment method at Meta.

**3. Your number, or ours.** A few questions decide the path: do you have a Meta Business account, a WhatsApp
Business app, a number of your own. You have a number: you connect it. You don't: “Provide me a number” gives you a
dedicated UK number, included in the Pro plan, and €3.50 a month without it.

For the provided number, you pay on Stripe's page (a promo code goes there, if we gave you one), then the number
shows. In Meta's window, choose “Enter a new phone number”, the country GB (+44), then the digits after +44;
**to get the code, choose “Phone call”, NOT “Text message”**: the code shows within seconds on the page, in the “The code will appear here” box, under your number. With no code after
45 seconds, request it again, still by “Phone call”. If Meta refuses this number, “Get another
one” gives you a new one.

**4. Meta's window.** There you choose your business account and your number, Meta sends you a code, you enter it,
and you go to the last screen (“Finish”).

No Meta Business account? The window has you create it along the way. If you had already started, starting again
works: you find the existing account and number. When Meta refuses, its own reason shows.

Sometimes Meta finishes without having verified the number: the number card says so, and “Activate the number” gets
you a code. Meta only tolerates ten code requests per number over 72 hours: the screen never retries on its own.

**5. Read the number's status.** The number card shows your WhatsApp profile picture, the account status as a
coloured badge, the number's quality, your display name and how many customers you can write to per 24 hours.

The number of customers reads “Not yet evaluated by Meta” until Meta decides. When a status cannot be read, the screen
says “Status unavailable” with a Retry button. Just below, the **WhatsApp Business account** panel shows Meta's
review of the account, your business verification and the owning business. With the Pro plan, four figures over 30
days open the page: contacts, messages exchanged, templates sent and estimated cost.

**Good to know.** For your workspace's first number, €1 of AI credit is offered as soon as Meta has verified it,
once. A workspace runs a single number: connecting a second one is refused, telling you which one is already there.
