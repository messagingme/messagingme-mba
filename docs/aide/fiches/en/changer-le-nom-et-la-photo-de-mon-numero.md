# Change the name and photo of my WhatsApp number

The name and photo your customers see on WhatsApp belong to your number at Meta, not to your Messaging Me
workspace. You change them in your Business Manager, in three clicks.

**Do you keep the WhatsApp Business app?** This guide does not work for your number: Meta leaves the name and photo
to the app, and they can be changed neither in Business Manager nor in Messaging Me. Change them in the app’s
business profile, on your phone. This guide applies to a number connected directly to the official API, without the
app.

**1. Open WhatsApp Manager.** In your Business Manager (business.facebook.com), click the ≡ menu at the bottom left:
“All tools” opens. Choose “WhatsApp Manager”.

You can also type its address: business.facebook.com/wa/manage/home.

**2. Select your number.** Further down the page, each WhatsApp account lists its numbers: click yours.

The left menu leads there too: “Account tools”, then “Phone numbers”.

**3. Change the name or the photo.** In the number’s “Profile” tab: “Edit” under “Display name” for the name, the
button under “Profile picture” for the photo.

The preview, on the right, shows what your customers will see. Meta reviews every new name, ten changes at most per
30-day period: during the review, or after a rejection, WhatsApp may show the number instead of the name. Pick the
name of your business or its brand: Meta rejects a name unrelated to it.

**Good to know.** Name approved by Meta? Write to us: Meta requires the number to be re-registered within 14 days for
the new name to show. Your Messaging Me workspace name never shows on WhatsApp.
