# Pay for your messages without a card at Meta

With the credit line, you have no card to add at Meta: Messaging Me pays Meta for your WhatsApp messages on your behalf,
and you prepay them with us, at an all-inclusive price. It is an option of the Pro and Business plans, at €30 excl. VAT
per number per month; on Enterprise, it follows your contract.

It applies to a number that does not keep the WhatsApp Business app: a number that keeps it is paid with your card at
Meta. The option is taken on the Pro plan from its trial, or on a confirmed Business plan; taken during the trial, it
stops with no refund if the Pro plan does not continue after the 14 days. Every step on this page is for the
workspace's administrators only.

**1. Take the option.** In **Settings > Plan**, **Credit line** tab, choose how many numbers, then “Take the option”:
Stripe’s payment page opens. The option is paid when taken, even during the Pro plan trial. The “Credit line” card of
the “Connect WhatsApp” page offers it too.

**2. Connect your number without a card.** On the “Connect WhatsApp” page, the “Credit line” card asks “Your number, or
ours?”. Your number: “I’ll use an existing number”, then “Connect my number”. Ours: “I need a number”, “Get my number”,
then “Open the Meta window”. Either way, Meta’s window opens without asking for a payment method.

“I’ll use an existing number”: your number, with the phone that holds it, where Meta sends a code. “I need a number”: a
number we provide, included in your plan. Your number’s line opens on connection, within 24 business hours at most.
From Claude Code, the connection link opens the same window, with no card.

Is your number already connected with your card at Meta? The credit line can’t take it over: to change this, write to
Support. To benefit from it, connect your number without a card from the start.

**3. Top up your credit line.** Its balance starts at zero: before your first templates, top it up in the **Credit line**
tab, by €50, €100, €250 or €500 excl. VAT, through Stripe’s payment page. The tab shows the price of a message to France,
category by category, all included. At zero, no message goes out any more, replies included.
