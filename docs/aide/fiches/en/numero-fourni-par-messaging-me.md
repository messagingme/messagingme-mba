# Get a number provided by Messaging Me

No number for WhatsApp? We provide a UK number, which Meta’s window connects to the official WhatsApp API: €3.50 excl.
VAT a month, included in the Pro plan. No Meta Business account? Meta’s window creates it along the way.

**1. Start the setup.** In Claude Code, type “/messagingme:demarrer”: Claude checks your workspace and starts with your
WhatsApp number. In the console, the “Connect WhatsApp” page asks the same questions.

**2. Answer Claude’s questions.** No Meta Business account, no WhatsApp Business app, no number: Claude offers you a
provided number.

The video stays on the Free plan: Meta bills your templates to a card, which you add once the number is connected. With
the WhatsApp Business app for another number, answer “No” to “Do you want to use that number?”: you reach the same
provided number.

**3. Pay for the number, then open Meta’s window.** Claude gives you the connection link. On the page, pay €3.50 excl.
VAT a month (with the Pro plan, “Get my number”, with nothing to pay): your UK number appears. Click “Open the Meta
window”.

The link is valid for one hour; once it has passed, Claude gives a new one. In the console, “Provide me a number” leads
to the same payment (on the Pro plan, to the included number). Keep the page open until the end: the verification code will show there.

**4. Connect the number in Meta’s window.** Log in with Facebook. On the number screen, enter a new number: the country
GB (+44), then the digits that follow +44. **For the code, tick “Phone call”, NOT “Text message”.**

After a security check, Meta calls the number: we pick up the call, and the code shows on the connection page and in
Claude, usually within seconds. Type it into Meta’s window.

No code after 30 seconds: ask Claude whether it got it. Still nothing after 45 seconds: wait until Meta offers to send
it again, and ask for it again, still by phone call.

If the window only offers “Text message”, take it: the text reaches us read aloud by a call, and the code usually shows
the same way. The video shows Meta’s window in French: there, these choices read “Appel téléphonique” and “Message
texte”.

**5. Create your Meta Business account.** Choose “Create a business portfolio”: if you already have one, even a
forgotten one, it is in the list, select it. Fill in your business, check your profile, then authorize Messaging Me.

For a new portfolio, Meta asks for your business name, an email address, its category, the country and your website.

**6. Finish.** “Your account is connected to Messaging Me”: finish without adding a payment method (“Ignorer et
terminer la configuration” in the French window of the video). The connection page and Claude then say “Your WhatsApp
number is connected”.

Meta’s card comes next, before your first templates: Claude gives you the steps.

**Good to know.** If Meta refuses this number, “Meta refuses this number? Get another one”, on the connection page,
gives you a new one.
